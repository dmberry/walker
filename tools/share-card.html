1200 × 630, opaque, JPEG. Both dimensions matter: 600 × 315 is the documented minimum for a large card but Messages and WhatsApp fall back to a small thumbnail below 1200 × 630, and an alpha channel makes both of them drop the preview outright. So: no transparency anywhere, and a JPEG rather than a PNG.

To rewrite the file, open this page and run document.getElementById('cv').toDataURL('image/jpeg', 0.86), then decode the base64 into assets/brand/share-card.jpg.
